# Check a risk allowance

Load when an estimate needs an uncertainty allowance or when costs may include the same risk twice.

1. Name the event, affected scope, evidence, timing and cost driver. Keep the party assigned a task separate from the party that bears cost under the adopted contract.
2. Separate known base cost, provisional sums, identified risk allowances, escalation, overhead and profit. Check existing rates and other packages for the same cost before adding anything.
3. Quantify only from supported quantities and costs or an explicit scenario. State the basis for any likelihood, range or percentage. If the basis is missing, keep the exposure unquantified and identify the evidence needed.
4. For a weighted scenario, show the likelihood and impact inputs. Do not present a sum of weighted values as a guaranteed budget, a confidence level or a contractual entitlement.
5. Test the material duration, frequency or access uncertainty with stated alternatives. Do not add overlapping alternatives together. Record shared causes before combining risk estimates.
6. Return the proposed allowance, cost base, exclusions, mitigation owner and review trigger. Keep it separate from submitted supplier prices and distinguish it from approved pricing policy.

The [reviewed guide](guide-provenance.json) supplies methods only. Its example percentages, UK prices and generic allocation examples are not Hong Kong defaults or adopted project rules.
