# Check a pricing anomaly

Load for a material rate, arithmetic or coverage discrepancy. Use the submitted quotation and the adopted tender requirements as evidence.

1. Retain the submitted rates, extensions, currency, date and total exactly as received. Recalculate arithmetic in a separate analysis column.
2. Compare the same scope, quantity unit, service period, frequency, ownership and inclusions. Keep system-month, station-month and point-month distinct until a supported conversion exists.
3. Record each discrepancy with its source location, calculation and commercial effect. A blank price remains unknown. A stated zero needs a coverage check before treating the work as included.
4. Show proposed comparison adjustments separately, with their source or `CALCULATION_ONLY` assumption. An arithmetic difference does not authorise changing the submitted tender price. Obtain the adopted correction procedure and required authority before any formal correction.
5. Investigate concentrated early costs, low recurring rates and material outliers against comparable scope. Do not infer misconduct, inability to perform or noncompliance from price alone. Do not invent a threshold for rejection.
6. Report the supported comparison subtotal, unpriced gaps and the clarification needed. Keep any ranking conditional where unresolved amounts could change it.

Use [guide provenance](guide-provenance.json) to inspect the reviewed methods and exclusions. This checklist does not supply contract rules or market rates.
