# Specialist cost and contract references

**Review date: 29 October 2026 (Hong Kong).** These are original workflow notes for
I&M, instrumented ground investigation and ground improvement QS work. Use the
[source register](source-register.json) for authority/access metadata. Read the precise
adopted page or clause before applying a measurement, payment or legal rule.

## Specialist price evidence

For GI instrument installation under adopted CEDD SMM Section 2, inspect clauses
2.27–2.30. Clause 2.30 includes calibration, maintenance, taking readings and providing
the associated records in the installation coverage. Separate costs for these resources
remain useful internally, but cannot establish separate payment. A project item or particular
preamble may establish a different payable structure; resolve its actual wording and amendments.

| Source ID | Reference | Appropriate treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `CEDD-CEWTPI` | [CEDD Civil Engineering Works Tender Price Index scope](https://www.cedd.gov.hk/eng/publications/standards-spec-handbooks-cost/CEWTPI-disclaimer/index.html) | It excludes ground investigation, E&M installations, architectural work, soft landscaping, ancillary work and term contracts. Do not apply it automatically to instrumented GI or a whole specialist package. |
| `CSD-CONSTRUCTION` | [Costs of labour index, Table 615-21001](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/web_table.html?id=615-21001) and [costs of materials index, Table 615-21002](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/web_table.html?id=615-21002) | Select the contract-specified series, base period and formula for fluctuations. The 2021- and 2003-based series are distinct; the ready-mixed-concrete series has its own April 2025 base. Do not mix bases. |

Obtain current equipment, hire, drilling, calibration, crew, telemetry and reporting prices
from project quotations or traceable resource evidence. Record scope, issue/expiry dates,
currency, freight, working hours, minimum charges and exclusions. These publications do
not provide a universal Hong Kong rate for a monitoring point, borehole or grouting operation.
An estimating index is not a substitute for a contractual price-adjustment formula.

## Payment-protection applicability

| Source ID | Reference | Application check |
|---|---|---|
| `DEVB-SOPO` | [Cap. 652](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap652), [CIC official scope](https://www.cic.hk/content/sopl/en/scope.html) and [FAQ](https://www.cic.hk/content/sopl/en/faq.html) | Check current law, entry date, main-contract scope/value, public/private exclusions and linked subcontract chain before relying on statutory rights/deadlines. The register ID is an internal key; the linked statute/CIC material identifies the actual publisher. |
| `DEVB-SOPP-2021` | [DEVB TC(W) 6/2021 transitional update](https://www.devb.gov.hk/filemanager/technicalcirculars/en/upload/386/1/C-2021-06-02.pdf) | Distinguish earlier contractual security-of-payment provisions from statutory Cap. 652; read the actual incorporated provisions and transitional arrangements. |

The principal Cap. 652 provisions became operative on 28 August 2025. This date alone
does not establish that a particular subcontract is covered. Review the date and coverage
of the relevant main contract, the type of work/supply and statutory exclusions.
For time-sensitive action, verify the exact provision, service facts, trigger, shorter
contractual period if relevant and day-counting rules. Do not apply remembered blanket
notice or payment periods, or treat the commercial amount claimed as admitted/approved.

## Output discipline

Keep four questions separate: **what work happened**, **how it is measured**, **whether
the contract/statute supports payment**, and **what amount the arithmetic produces**.
Record supporting clause/page, edition, incorporation and evidence against each conclusion.
Use `NEEDS_SOURCE` for an identified missing input, `CALCULATION_ONLY` for unsupported
scenarios and `READY_FOR_QS_REVIEW` for supported drafts. Missing data is not zero.
Draft correspondence for the authorised person; do not issue notices or certificates.
Do not redistribute proprietary source books or contracts, or present these notes as their text.
