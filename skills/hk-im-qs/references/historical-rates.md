# Compare historical rates

Load when a historical cost or rate is proposed as evidence for the current estimate.

1. Record the source, price date, currency, location, procurement basis and whether the amount is a quote, tender, paid cost or final account. Preserve the original value.
2. Compare scope, technical specification, quantity scale, billing denominator, inclusions, service period, frequency, access and ownership. Do not average station-month and system-month rates or blend supply with rental without a supported conversion.
3. Select comparable records and state exclusions. Keep a weak comparator visible as a limitation instead of forcing it into the estimate. One record does not establish a market range.
4. Show every adjustment separately with its formula, source and applicability. Use a time index, exchange rate or location factor only where its basis fits the component and dates. Do not import UK indices, wages, tax or location factors by default.
5. If no applicable adjustment evidence exists, show the unadjusted record with its limits or use a labelled scenario. Do not invent a current Hong Kong rate.
6. Report the comparable records, adjusted comparison values, spread and unresolved differences. Explain an outlier before excluding it. Keep historical comparisons separate from the current supplier's submitted price and from any contractual entitlement.

Use [guide provenance](guide-provenance.json) for the reviewed methods. Guide examples and statistical cut-offs are not mandatory acceptance limits.
