# Assess a change, variation or monitoring extension

Assess the proposed change against the original obligations. Route an ordinary budget sensitivity to estimating when the user does not ask about entitlement, instructions or a claim.

## Inputs

Read [project basis](../references/project-basis.md), [valuation rules](../references/valuation-rules.md), [cost/contract sources](../references/cost-contract-sources.md) and the relevant domain guidance. Obtain the priced baseline, scope/measurement inclusions, actual contract/amendments, instruction/event records, issuer/authority, programme impact, notices and records of resources or service periods. Continue supported quantity/cost analysis while isolating missing contractual evidence.

## Steps

1. **Define the alleged change.** State the event, affected IDs/zones/periods, original requirement, revised requirement, cause and instruction date/source. Separate added points, relocations, higher frequency, extended monitoring, extra visits, standby/remobilisation and re-treatment instead of treating them as one undifferentiated extra.
2. **Test existing coverage first.** Compare the change with original installation, calibration, baseline, monitoring, maintenance, defects and end obligations. A longer period or repeated treatment is not automatically extra payment; identify the adopted end trigger, cause, performance obligation and actual change mechanism. Maintain a distinction between doing the work, pricing it and establishing entitlement.
3. **Apply the actual contract route.** Use the adopted GCC, NEC options/amendments or bespoke subcontract provisions. Identify authority, event/variation category, notice recipient, method, deadline calculation, submission records and current response status from actual text. Do not invent time bars, assume every instruction is valid or transplant main-contract clauses into a subcontract. Mark a missed/unclear step as an issue for review with its evidence, not a conclusive legal judgment based on a generic rule.
4. **Quantify the delta once.** Reuse the baseline ledger and use [measure-quantities](measure-quantities.md) where needed. Split additional service periods by accepted IDs, dates, stages and denominators. Separate gross requested cost, omitted/replaced work, duplicated allowances, mitigation and residual delta. Keep failed/repeated work and its cause visible.
5. **Price supported scenarios.** Use [estimate-and-compare-quotes](estimate-and-compare-quotes.md) for the applicable existing/new rate or resource build-up. Show one-off, recurring and event costs, ownership/shared-resource allocation and extension assumptions. Keep assessed entitlement and priced scenarios in separate fields; pass only the appropriate supported/claimed category to a valuation.
6. **Check and deliver the assessment.** Reconcile quantities, dates and rates to original and revised scope; verify the event/notice chronology against source documents. Return the assessment and a draft notice/VO/RFI if requested. Draft an urgent evidence-based notice using available facts when authorised to prepare one, with unresolved particulars identified; do not delay a useful draft for an unrelated full-project intake. Sending still requires explicit user authority.

## Outputs

Provide `event ID | baseline vs revised scope | contractual route/source | instruction and notice chronology | quantity delta | pricing basis | amount | entitlement/evidence position | next action`. Include a supporting build-up and requested draft correspondence. Apply `CALCULATION_ONLY`, `NEEDS_SOURCE` or `READY_FOR_QS_REVIEW` to each conclusion; a checkable price may remain `CALCULATION_ONLY` while entitlement is `NEEDS_SOURCE`.

## Completion checks

- The original duty and actual changed requirement are both evidenced or explicitly unresolved.
- Contract route, incorporation and notice evidence are specific to the package; no invented deadline, entitlement or deemed acceptance is embedded.
- Added work, omissions, overlapping periods, shared resources and previous claims reconcile without double counting.
- The cost scenario is visibly distinct from the entitlement position and any supported valuation amount.
- Missing evidence has a concrete owner/question and an identified commercial effect where quantifiable.
- Any notice/application remains a draft unless issuance was explicitly authorised; no technical release or payment certification is claimed.
