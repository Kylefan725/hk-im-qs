# Hong Kong I&M measurement and technical source map

Verified **29 October 2026**. This map serves instrumentation and monitoring, associated ground investigation and instrumented ground improvement. Sources have different purposes; their appearance here does not incorporate them into a contract. See [source-register.json](source-register.json) for official URLs, editions and access/verification limits.

## Important commercial distinction

Where adopted without a relevant amendment, SMM 2.27–2.30 treats GI instrument installation by number and includes specified calibration, maintenance, readings and records within its coverage. Check that full coverage and the particular preambles before adding charges. The source is the [official consolidated SMM](https://www.cedd.gov.hk/filemanager/eng/content_79/cesmm_rev_10.pdf), not this summary.

This is a specific reference route for GI installations. Do not turn it into a universal measurement rule for every modern ADMS, wireless sensor or data-service package. Read the actual BOQ/particular schedule and resolve its defined scope and denominator.

Internal cost components can be separated for estimating even where one inclusive bill item is payable. Maintain a cost-to-bill crosswalk so components are covered once. Conversely, a required technical activity may be unpriced or disputed; mark the question instead of automatically treating it as an extra.

## Edition and applicability checks

- CEDD SMM is the **1992 Edition**, with its listed consolidated version incorporating **Corrigendum 1/2011**. Preserve the adopted edition and particular preambles.
- CEDD's GS catalogue lists the **2020 Edition**, with the inspected Volume 1 Rev.8 incorporating **Amendment 3/2026**. Verify which amendments the project incorporates; the latest web file does not amend a signed contract by itself.
- GS and SMM section numbering differ. GS **Section 7 Part 2** concerns GI and **Part 6** instrumentation; SMM **Section 2** concerns GI. Do not cross-apply clause numbers.
- A technical specification establishes performance/scope. The contract and measurement documents establish payment treatment. A guidance note supports the appropriate technical context.
- Use the particular monitoring plan to define location, accuracy, stage, frequency, trigger/action requirements and roles. These skills supply no numerical engineering defaults.

## Obtain and preserve evidence

Use official links or lawfully supplied project copies. For every decision retain title, edition/revision, clause/printed page, applicable project document, input records and an original concise explanation. Retrieve only the necessary text. Keep full PDFs, extracted text and confidential project records out of the distributed repository.

Use [GI references](ground-investigation-sources.md), [ground-improvement references](ground-improvement-sources.md) and [cost/contract references](cost-contract-sources.md) when those branches are needed. A catalogue entry verifies metadata only; it cannot establish detailed rules. Continue supported quantities with targeted `NEEDS_SOURCE` entries for gaps.
